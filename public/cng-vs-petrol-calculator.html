Example: Daily Office Commute

You drive 1,200 km a month (about 40 km a day). On petrol your car gives 15 km per litre at ₹102, which costs ₹6.80 per km or ₹8,160 a month. On CNG it gives 22 km per kg at ₹81, which costs ₹3.68 per km or ₹4,418 a month. You save about ₹3,742 a month, so a ₹55,000 kit pays for itself in about 15 months.

When CNG Makes Sense

  • You drive a lot. The more kilometres per month, the faster the kit or CNG premium pays back. Taxi and daily commuters benefit most.
  • CNG is easily available on your routes. CNG stations are common in Delhi-NCR, Mumbai, Pune, Gujarat and some other cities, but scarce in many areas, and queues can be long.
  • You can spare the boot space. The cylinder takes up much of the boot in most cars, although some newer models use twin smaller cylinders.

Things to Keep in Mind

  • Engines make a little less power on CNG, which you notice on hills and when fully loaded.
  • An aftermarket kit must be fitted by an approved installer and recorded on your RC by the RTO. An unendorsed kit can cause problems with insurance claims.
  • The CNG cylinder needs periodic hydro-testing, and the kit needs regular servicing. Budget a small amount for this.
  • CNG prices change from time to time and differ by city, so check your local rate before deciding.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is CNG cheaper than petrol per km?

Usually yes, by around 40 to 50 percent. A car giving 15 km per litre on petrol at ₹102 costs about ₹6.80 per km, while the same class of car giving 22 km per kg on CNG at ₹81 costs about ₹3.68 per km.

How long does a CNG kit take to pay back?

It depends on how much you drive. At 1,200 km a month, a ₹55,000 kit saving about ₹3,700 a month pays back in roughly 15 months. If you drive only 400 km a month, it takes about three times as long.

Does CNG reduce car performance?

Engines produce somewhat less power on CNG, and the cylinder takes up boot space. Factory-fitted CNG cars are tuned for it and keep their warranty. Aftermarket kits must be fitted by an approved installer and endorsed on the RC with the RTO.
