How Zakat Is Calculated

  1. Add your zakatable assets: cash, bank balances, gold, silver, business stock, business cash, investments and money owed to you that you expect to receive.
  2. Subtract debts that are due now.
  3. Compare with the nisab, the value of 612.36 g of silver or 87.48 g of gold.
  4. If your net wealth is at or above the nisab, and you have held wealth above the nisab for one lunar year, zakat is 2.5% of the net amount.

Example

Suppose you have ₹2,00,000 in the bank, 20 g of 22K gold jewellery, 100 g of silver and ₹30,000 of bills due. With 24K gold at ₹13,500 per gram and silver at ₹170 per gram:

  • 22K gold: 20 g × ₹12,375 (22/24 of ₹13,500) = ₹2,47,500
  • Silver: 100 g × ₹170 = ₹17,000
  • Total assets: ₹4,64,500. Minus ₹30,000 debts = ₹4,34,500
  • Silver nisab: 612.36 g × ₹170 = about ₹1,04,100, so zakat is due.
  • Zakat: 2.5% × ₹4,34,500 = ₹10,862.50

What Is Not Counted

  • The house you live in, your car, furniture, clothes and household items.
  • Tools and equipment you use for work, and business premises.
  • Property you own to live in or rent out (rental income you have saved is counted as cash).

Who Can Receive Zakat

The Qur'an (Surah At-Tawbah, 9:60) names eight groups, including the poor, the needy, those in debt, and travellers in difficulty. Zakat cannot be given to your parents, grandparents, children or grandchildren, or by a husband to his wife. Many people give through a trusted local masjid, madrasa or relief organisation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the nisab for zakat in India?

The nisab is the value of 87.48 grams of gold or 612.36 grams of silver. In rupees it changes with metal prices: at about ₹170 per gram of silver, the silver nisab is about ₹1,04,100, and at about ₹13,500 per gram of 24K gold, the gold nisab is about ₹11,81,000.

Should I use the gold or silver nisab?

Many Hanafi scholars, whose school most Muslims in India follow, advise using the silver nisab when your wealth is cash or a mix of assets, because it is lower and benefits more people in need. If you only own gold, the gold nisab applies. Ask your local scholar if you are unsure.

Is zakat due on gold jewellery worn by women?

In the Hanafi school, zakat is due on all gold and silver, including jewellery worn regularly. The Shafi'i, Maliki and Hanbali schools generally exempt jewellery kept for permissible personal use. Follow the view of your school or local scholar.

How much zakat do I have to pay?

2.5% of your net zakatable wealth, if it is at or above the nisab and you have held wealth above the nisab for one lunar (Islamic) year. For example, net zakatable wealth of ₹4,34,500 gives zakat of about ₹10,863.
