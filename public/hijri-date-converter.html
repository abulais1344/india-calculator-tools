About the Hijri Calendar

The Hijri (Islamic) calendar is a lunar calendar of 12 months with 29 or 30 days each, so a year has 354 or 355 days. It begins from the Hijrah, the migration of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ from Makkah to Madinah in 622 CE. Because it is about 11 days shorter than the Gregorian year, Ramadan and Eid move about 11 days earlier every year.

This converter uses calendars built into your browser: Umm al-Qura, the official calendar of Saudi Arabia, and the tabular (civil) calendar. In India, months begin after local moon sighting, which often falls one day later than Umm al-Qura, so use the −1 day adjustment to match most Indian calendars.

Frequently Asked Questions

When does Ramadan 2027 start?

By the Umm al-Qura calendar, 1 Ramadan 1448 falls on 8 February 2027, and Eid al-Fitr (1 Shawwal) on 9 March 2027. In India the dates depend on local moon sighting and are often one day later.

Why do Hijri dates in India differ from Saudi Arabia?

Each Islamic month begins with the sighting of the new crescent moon. India's moon-sighting committees often see the moon a day after Saudi Arabia, so Indian Hijri dates are frequently one day behind the Umm al-Qura calendar. Use the adjustment option to match your local calendar.

How many days are in a Hijri year?

A Hijri year has 354 or 355 days, about 11 days fewer than the Gregorian year, so Ramadan and Eid move about 11 days earlier each year.
