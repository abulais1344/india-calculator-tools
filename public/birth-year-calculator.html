How the Year Calculator Works

Birth year from age: subtract your age from the current year. If your birthday has already happened this year, that is your birth year. If not, you were born one year earlier. For example, if you are 25 in 2026, you were born in 2001 if your birthday has passed, or 2000 if it has not.

Age from birth year: subtract your birth year from the target year to get the age you turn on your birthday that year. For example, someone born in 1995 turns 31 in 2026 and is 30 until their birthday.

Years between two years: subtract the earlier year from the later one. For example, from 1947 to 2026 is 79 years.

Because a year alone does not say whether your birthday has passed, results that depend on it show both possible answers. For an exact age on a specific date, such as an exam cut-off, use the Age As On Date Calculator.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find my birth year from my age?

Subtract your age from the current year. If your birthday has already passed this year, that is your birth year. If your birthday is still to come, subtract one more year. For example, someone who is 25 in 2026 was born in 2001 if their birthday has passed, or in 2000 if it has not.

How old will I be in a given year?

Subtract your birth year from the target year. That is the age you turn on your birthday in that year. Before your birthday you are one year younger. For example, someone born in 1995 turns 31 in 2026.

Why does the calculator show two possible answers?

Age and year alone cannot tell whether your birthday has already happened this year. The answer differs by one year depending on that, so both possibilities are shown. For an exact answer, use the Age Calculator with your full date of birth.
