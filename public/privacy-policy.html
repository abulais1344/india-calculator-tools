Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use CalcVerse (calcverse.in), how it is used, and the choices you have. By using this website, you agree to this policy.

1. Information You Enter in Calculators

All calculators and generators on CalcVerse run entirely in your web browser. The numbers, dates and other details you type (for example your date of birth, salary, electricity units or gold weight) are not sent to our servers, not stored by us, and not shared with anyone. When you close or refresh the page, they are gone. CalcVerse does not require you to sign up or create an account.

Like most websites, our hosting provider (Vercel) automatically records basic technical information when you visit a page, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, the page requested and the time of the request. This is used to deliver the website, keep it secure and fix errors. We do not use it to identify you personally.

  • If you opt out, you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you.

To learn how Google uses information from websites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.
