Frequently Asked Questions

How many square feet are in 1 acre?

1 acre = 43,560 square feet = 4,840 square yards (gaj) = 4,046.86 square metres = 100 cents = 40 guntas.

How many square feet are in 1 bigha?

It depends on the state. Commonly cited values are about 14,400 sq ft in West Bengal and Assam, 17,424 sq ft in Gujarat, 27,000 sq ft in Uttar Pradesh, 27,220 sq ft in Bihar, 9,070 sq ft in Punjab and 8,712 sq ft in Himachal Pradesh. Sizes can differ even within a state, so confirm with local land records.

How many square feet is 1 gunta and 1 cent?

1 gunta = 1,089 sq ft (1/40 acre), used in Karnataka, Maharashtra and Telangana. 1 cent = 435.6 sq ft (1/100 acre), used in Kerala, Tamil Nadu and Andhra Pradesh.
