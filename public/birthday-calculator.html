How to Use the Birthday Calculator

Enter your date of birth and press Calculate Birthday. The calculator instantly shows:

  • Days until your next birthday, counted exactly from today.
  • The date and weekday of your next birthday, so you can plan a celebration or a day off.
  • The age you will turn on that birthday.
  • The day of the week you were born, such as Monday or Saturday.
  • Your next 5 birthdays with the weekday each one falls on.

You can also fill in the optional Count From date to see the countdown from any other day. This is handy when you are planning a party or booking travel in advance.

Example

Suppose you were born on 15 August 2000. That was a Tuesday. Counting from 11 November 2026, your next birthday is on 15 August 2027, a Sunday. That is 277 days away, and you will turn 27. By 11 November 2026 you would have lived 9,584 days.

Birthdays on 29 February

If you were born on 29 February, your exact birthday only comes once every four years, in a leap year. In other years, most people celebrate on 28 February or 1 March. This calculator uses 28 February in non-leap years and the real 29 February in leap years. For example, someone born on 29 February 2004 (a Sunday) celebrates on 28 February 2027 and has a true birthday again on 29 February 2028, a Tuesday.

How the Countdown Is Calculated

The calculator takes your birth day and month and places them in the current year. If that date has already passed, it moves to next year. It then counts the calendar days between today and that date, allowing for months of different lengths and leap years. Because everything runs in your browser, the result is instant and your date of birth is never stored or sent anywhere.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many days are left until my birthday?

Enter your date of birth in the birthday calculator. It counts the exact number of days from today to your next birthday, including leap years, and also shows the date and weekday of that birthday.

How do I find which day of the week I was born?

Enter your date of birth and the calculator shows the weekday you were born on. For example, 15 August 2000 was a Tuesday.

When do people born on 29 February celebrate their birthday?

29 February only exists in leap years. In other years most people celebrate on 28 February or 1 March. This calculator uses 28 February in non-leap years and shows the real 29 February date in leap years.

Is this birthday calculator free and private?

Yes. It is free, needs no login, and all calculations run in your browser. Your date of birth is not stored or sent anywhere.
