How the Calculation Works

  • Backup hours = battery Ah × battery voltage × efficiency ÷ load in watts. Battery voltage is 12V per battery (24V for two).
  • Inverter size (VA) = load watts ÷ 0.8 power factor, rounded up to a standard size.
  • Battery Ah needed = load watts × hours ÷ (voltage × efficiency).

Frequently Asked Questions

How long will a 150Ah inverter battery last?

A 150Ah, 12V battery running a 300 W load (for example 3 fans and 5 LED lights) lasts about 4.8 hours, using backup hours = Ah × volts × 0.8 ÷ load watts. At 500 W it lasts under 3 hours.

What size inverter do I need?

Add up the watts of everything you want to run at the same time and divide by 0.8 (typical power factor). A 600 W load needs about a 750 VA inverter; choose the next standard size up, such as 900 VA or 1100 VA.

Can an inverter run an AC or geyser?

Usually not a normal home inverter. ACs, geysers, irons and induction cooktops draw 1,000–2,000 W or more and would drain the battery in minutes. Use the inverter for fans, lights, TV, router and the fridge.
