How to Use the Date Calculator

  • Days between dates: pick two dates to see the number of days, weeks, months and working days between them, for example days left until an exam, a notice period or a due date.
  • Add or subtract: find the date a number of days, weeks, months or years before or after a date, for example "90 days from today" for a deadline or a warranty end date.
  • Day of the week: find which weekday any date falls on, past or future.

Examples

  • From 24 November 2026 to 31 March 2027 is 127 days (18 weeks and 1 day), with 92 weekdays counting both dates.
  • 100 days after 24 November 2026 is Thursday, 4 March 2027.
  • Adding 1 month to 31 January gives the last day of February (28 or 29 February), because February has no 31st.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I count the days between two dates?

Subtract the earlier date from the later one. For example, from 24 November 2026 to 31 March 2027 is 127 days (128 if you count both the start and end dates).

What date is 100 days from today?

Use the Add or Subtract tab: choose the start date, enter 100 days, and the calculator shows the resulting date and weekday. For example, 100 days after 24 November 2026 is Thursday, 4 March 2027.

How are working days counted?

Working days count Monday to Friday (or Monday to Saturday if you choose a 6-day week) between the two dates, including both dates. Public holidays are not removed, so subtract those separately.
