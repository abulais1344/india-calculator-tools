Why Solar Output Varies

  • Location: western and southern India get more strong sunshine than the hills and the North-East.
  • Season: output is highest in March to May and lowest in the monsoon. In North India, winter haze also cuts generation.
  • Shade and angle: even partial shade from a tank or tree lowers output. Panels should face south and be kept clean of dust.
  • Panel age: panels lose a little output each year, usually under 1%.

Over a full year these effects average out, which is why about 4 units per kW per day is the common planning figure.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many units does a 1 kW solar panel generate per day in India?

About 4 units (kWh) per day on average, which is roughly 120 units a month and 1,400 to 1,500 units a year. It is higher in sunny states like Rajasthan and Gujarat and lower in winter, monsoon and hilly areas.

How many units does a 3 kW solar system generate per month?

About 360 units a month at the Indian average of 4 units per kW per day, or about 12 units a day.

How many solar panels are needed for 1 kW?

Two 550 W panels make 1.1 kW, which is the common way to build a 1 kW home system. With older 330 W panels you would need about 3 to 4 panels.
