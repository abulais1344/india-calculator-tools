CGPA to Percentage Formulas

  • CBSE (Class 10 grading): Percentage = CGPA × 9.5. NIT Delhi also uses 9.5.
  • AICTE formula: Percentage = (CGPA − 0.75) × 10, used by many engineering and management colleges.
  • CGPA × 10: used by some universities, such as Anna University.

How CGPA Is Calculated From SGPA

CGPA = Σ (SGPA × credits) ÷ Σ credits. For example, SGPAs of 8.2 (22 credits), 7.8 (24 credits) and 8.6 (20 credits) give (180.4 + 187.2 + 172) ÷ 66 = 8.18 CGPA. If every semester had the same credits, it would simply be the average.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert CGPA to percentage?

It depends on your board or university. CBSE uses CGPA × 9.5, so 8.4 CGPA is 79.8%. Many engineering colleges use the AICTE formula (CGPA − 0.75) × 10, which gives 76.5% for 8.4. Some universities, such as Anna University, use CGPA × 10. Always use the formula printed on your marksheet or published by your university.

How is CGPA calculated from SGPA?

CGPA is the credit-weighted average of your semester SGPAs: multiply each semester's SGPA by its total credits, add them up, and divide by the total credits of all semesters. If every semester has the same credits, it is simply the average of the SGPAs.

What is 9 CGPA in percentage?

85.5% using the CBSE formula (9 × 9.5), 82.5% using the AICTE formula ((9 − 0.75) × 10), or 90% where the university multiplies by 10.
