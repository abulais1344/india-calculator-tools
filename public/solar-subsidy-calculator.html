PM Surya Ghar Subsidy Rates

PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana gives central financial assistance to households installing grid-connected rooftop solar:

CapacityMost statesSpecial category states
First 2 kW₹30,000 per kW₹33,000 per kW
3rd kW₹18,000₹19,800
Maximum (3 kW and above)₹78,000₹85,800

Special category states and UTs are the North-Eastern states, Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh, Andaman & Nicobar and Lakshadweep. Group housing societies and RWAs can get ₹18,000 per kW for common facilities, which this calculator does not cover.

How to Apply

  1. Register on the national portal pmsuryaghar.gov.in with your state, DISCOM and electricity consumer number.
  2. Apply for rooftop solar and wait for feasibility approval from your DISCOM.
  3. Get the system installed by a vendor registered with your DISCOM.
  4. After installation, submit the plant details and apply for the net meter. The DISCOM inspects it and issues a commissioning certificate.
  5. Submit your bank details and a cancelled cheque on the portal. The subsidy is paid directly into your bank account.

Rates and procedures can change, and the scheme currently runs until 31 March 2027. Always confirm on the official portal before you buy.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is the PM Surya Ghar subsidy for a 3 kW solar system?

₹78,000 in most states: ₹30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW (₹60,000) plus ₹18,000 for the third kW. In special category states it is ₹85,800. This is the maximum central subsidy; systems larger than 3 kW get the same ₹78,000.

Who is eligible for the PM Surya Ghar subsidy?

Indian households with a valid residential electricity connection and a suitable roof, installing a grid-connected rooftop system through a registered vendor on the national portal (pmsuryaghar.gov.in), and who have not already taken a solar subsidy for the same connection.

Do states give extra solar subsidy?

Some states add their own subsidy on top of the central amount, and the rates change from time to time. Check your state's renewable energy agency or DISCOM website, then enter the extra amount in this calculator.
