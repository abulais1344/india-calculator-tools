How the Attendance Calculator Works

  • Attendance % = attended ÷ total × 100.
  • Classes needed to reach the target (if you are below it): attend the next x classes in a row, where x = (target × total − 100 × attended) ÷ (100 − target), rounded up.
  • Classes you can skip (if you are above it): y = (100 × attended ÷ target) − total, rounded down.

Examples at 75%

  • 30 out of 45 (66.67%): attend the next 15 classes in a row to reach 45 out of 60 = 75%.
  • 40 out of 45 (88.89%): you can skip the next 8 classes and stay at 40 out of 53 = 75.47%.

Most Indian universities and AICTE colleges require at least 75% attendance to sit for exams, sometimes with medical relaxation. Check your own college's rule.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate my attendance percentage?

Attendance % = classes attended ÷ total classes held × 100. For example, 30 classes attended out of 45 is 66.67%.

How many classes do I need to attend to reach 75%?

Use: classes needed = (0.75 × total − attended) ÷ 0.25, rounded up. With 30 out of 45, you need to attend the next 15 classes in a row to reach exactly 75% (45 out of 60).

How many classes can I skip and stay above 75%?

Use: classes you can skip = attended ÷ 0.75 − total, rounded down. With 40 out of 45, you can miss the next 8 classes and still have at least 75% (40 out of 53).
