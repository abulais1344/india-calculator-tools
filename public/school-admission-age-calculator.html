Admission Age Under NEP 2020

The National Education Policy 2020 sets a 5+3+3+4 structure: three years of pre-school before Class 1. The resulting minimum ages are:

Nursery / Pre-school 13 years31 March 2024

Delhi's Directorate of Education applies the 6+ rule for Class 1 from the 2026-27 session, and schools there may allow a relaxation of up to one month. Kendriya Vidyalayas also check age as on 31 March. Some states still use 5+ for Class 1 or a different cut-off date.

Example

A child born on 10 May 2021 will be 5 years, 10 months and 21 days old on 31 March 2027. Under the NEP 6+ rule they can join UKG in 2027-28 and Class 1 in 2028-29. A child born on 31 March 2021 turns exactly 6 on the cut-off date and is eligible for Class 1 in 2027-28.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum age for Class 1 admission in India?

Under NEP 2020, a child should be 6 years old to join Class 1, after three years of pre-school (Nursery at 3+, LKG at 4+, UKG at 5+). Delhi applies the 6+ rule from the 2026-27 session. Some states and schools still use 5+ or a different cut-off date, so always check the school's admission notice.

What is the cut-off date for school admission age?

Most schools, including Delhi schools and Kendriya Vidyalayas, check age as on 31 March of the admission year. Some states and boards use other dates such as 1 June or 30 September. Enter your school's date in the calculator.

My child turns 6 after 31 March. Can they join Class 1?

Not under a 6+ rule with a 31 March cut-off: the child must have completed 6 years on that date. Delhi allows school heads a relaxation of up to one month, and other states have their own rules, so ask the school.
