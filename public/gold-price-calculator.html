How Gold Jewellery Price Is Calculated

  1. Gold value = weight × rate for that purity. 22K gold is 91.6% pure, so its rate is about 91.6% of the 24K rate (jewellers' board rates can differ slightly).
  2. Making charges are added: usually 8–25% of the gold value, or a fixed amount per gram, depending on the design.
  3. GST of 3% is added. CBIC guidance treats jewellery as one supply taxed at 3% on the whole value, but some jewellers bill 5% on making charges separately, so check your invoice.

Example

10 g of 22K jewellery at a 24K rate of ₹13,500/g: gold value ₹1,23,660 + 12% making ₹14,839 = ₹1,38,499, plus 3% GST ₹4,155 = ₹1,42,654.

Tips When Buying Gold

  • Buy only BIS hallmarked jewellery with a 6-digit HUID code, and check it on the BIS Care app.
  • Ask for the gold rate, weight, making charges and GST to be shown separately on the bill.
  • Making charges are usually lost when you sell or exchange, so plain designs and coins hold value better.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the price of gold jewellery calculated?

Price = (gold rate for the purity × weight) + making charges + GST. For 10 g of 22K gold at a 24K rate of ₹13,500 per gram, the gold value is about ₹1,23,660; with 12% making charges and 3% GST on the total, the bill is about ₹1,42,654.

How much GST is charged on gold jewellery?

3% GST applies to gold. According to CBIC guidance, 3% applies to the whole jewellery value even when making charges are shown separately, but some jewellers charge 5% GST on making charges. Check how your invoice is billed.

How many grams are in 1 tola?

1 tola = 11.6638 grams. Jewellers often round it to 11.66 g; in some places a 10 g "tola" is used for convenience.
