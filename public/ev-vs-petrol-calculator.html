Examples

Electric car: you drive 1,200 km a month. A petrol car giving 15 km per litre at ₹102 costs ₹6.80 per km, or ₹8,160 a month. An electric car covering 7 km per unit at ₹8 per unit costs ₹1.14 per km, or ₹1,371 a month. You save about ₹6,789 a month, so a ₹3 lakh higher EV price pays back in about 3.7 years.

Electric scooter: you ride 900 km a month. A 45 km/l petrol bike costs about ₹2,040 a month, while a scooter covering 30 km per unit costs about ₹240. That saves about ₹1,800 a month, so a ₹40,000 higher price pays back in under 2 years.

What This Calculator Leaves Out

  • Maintenance: EVs have no engine oil, clutch or exhaust, so servicing usually costs less. This makes the real saving larger.
  • Battery: batteries usually come with a long warranty (often 8 years for cars), but a replacement outside warranty is expensive.
  • Insurance and resale: these vary by model and are not included.
  • Charging mix: if you often use public fast chargers, enter a blended rate instead of your home tariff.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the running cost of an electric car per km in India?

About ₹1 to ₹1.50 per km when charging at home. An electric car that covers about 7 km per unit of electricity at ₹8 per unit costs about ₹1.14 per km, compared with about ₹6.80 per km for a petrol car giving 15 km per litre at ₹102.

How much does it cost to charge an electric scooter?

Very little at home. A scooter that covers about 30 km per unit costs around 27 paise per km at ₹8 per unit, so 900 km a month costs about ₹240, against about ₹2,040 on petrol for a 45 km/l bike.

Is public fast charging cheaper than petrol?

Usually yes, but the gap is smaller. Public DC fast charging often costs ₹18 to ₹25 per unit, which can make an electric car cost ₹3 to ₹4 per km. Enter your charging rate in the calculator to compare.
