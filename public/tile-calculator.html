Example

A 12 × 10 ft room is 120 sq ft. With 2 × 2 ft tiles (4 sq ft each) you need 30 tiles, or 33 tiles with 10% extra. At 4 tiles per box, buy 9 boxes.

Tips

  • Add 5–10% extra for straight layouts and 10–15% for diagonal layouts or rooms with many corners.
  • For walls, subtract doors and windows from the area, or enter only the tiled height (for example 7 ft in a bathroom).
  • Buy all boxes from the same batch so the shade matches, and keep a few spares.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many 2x2 tiles are needed for a 12x10 room?

A 12 × 10 ft room is 120 sq ft. Each 2 × 2 ft tile covers 4 sq ft, so you need 30 tiles, or 33 with 10% extra for cutting and breakage. At 4 tiles per box, that is 9 boxes.

How many sq ft is a 600x600 mm tile?

A 600 × 600 mm tile covers about 3.875 sq ft (0.36 m²). An 800 × 800 mm tile covers about 6.89 sq ft, and a 600 × 1200 mm tile about 7.75 sq ft.

How much extra tile should I buy?

Usually 5–10% extra for cutting and breakage, and 10–15% for diagonal layouts or rooms with many corners. Keep a few spare tiles for future repairs, since the same shade can be hard to find later.
